Executive Leadership

Our leadership team combines investment and finance expertise, strategic direction and disciplined oversight across the firm and its investments.

Refilwe Sebego-Walebowa

Managing Director

Qualifications:

Bachelor of Applied Finance, Macquarie University| MSc Finance, Warwick Business School| FMVA, Corporate Finance Institute| Data Science & Business Analytics, McCombs Business School|

18+ years' experience across asset management, private equity, private debt and development finance. Refilwe is the chief executive responsible for providing strategic leadership, leads the overall implementation of the firm and investment strategy, performance, governance, and sustainability.

Prior to co-founding Mmathari Partners, Refilwe served as an Associate Partner at a private equity firm. Her earlier career includes senior investment roles at Botswana Development Corporation, Norsad Capital and Botswana Insurance Fund Management.

She has originated, evaluated, structured and executed investment transactions across multiple sectors throughout the SADC region, working with businesses at different stages of growth.

Refilwe brings extensive board and corporate governance experience across the reinsurance, healthcare, education and life insurance sectors and currently chairs the board of Liberty Life Botswana.

Refilwe holds a Bachelor of Applied Finance from Macquarie University in Australia, post graduate qualifications including Master of Science in Finance from Warwick Business School in the UK, Post-Graduate Program in Data Science & Business Analytics from McCombs Business School in USA and a certification ins Financial Modelling and Valuation Analysis (FMVA) from the Corporate Finance Institute.

Keboetswe Letsholo, CFA

Chief Investment Officer

Qualifications:

Bachelor of Finance, University of Botswana| CFA Charterholder, CFA Institute, USA|

10+ years of investment management experience. Keboetswe oversees the investment team and leads the implementation of the firm’s investment strategy.

She is responsible for sourcing, evaluating, executing, and monitoring investment opportunities, bringing more than a decade of end-to-end investment management experience across the Africa region.

Prior to co-founding Mmathari, Keboetswe served as an Associate Regional Representative at US-based impact fund manager Developing World Markets (DWM), where she was responsible for investments across Africa and the Middle East.

Before joining DWM, she held investment management roles at Norsad Capital, focusing on the structuring and deployment of private debt transactions throughout Sub-Saharan Africa. She has structured and managed debt, mezzanine and quasi-equity investments across all stages of the investment cycle. Her experience spans a diverse range of sectors and business maturities, from early-stage start-ups to mature businesses..

Keboetswe holds a Bachelor of Finance degree (first class) from the University of Botswana and is a CFA Charterholder.
