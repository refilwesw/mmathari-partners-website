Investment Committee

The Investment Committee is responsible for reviewing, evaluating and approving investment opportunities in line with the firm's investment strategy, governance framework and risk appetite. Through disciplined analysis and independent judgement, the Committee ensures investment decisions create sustainable value for investors and stakeholders.
