Who We Are

At Mmathari Partners, we believe sustainable investment is built on strong partnerships, disciplined execution and a deep understanding of local markets together with long-term local and global relationships.

Our experienced professionals work alongside target company management and stakeholders to identify opportunities, manage risk and deliver measurable impact.

Our Services

We provide disciplined investment expertise, strategic partnerships and responsible stewardship to create sustainable value for investors, businesses and institutions.

Private Markets Investment

We invest in high-potential and scalable private businesses across sectors, providing patient capital, strategic guidance and active partnership to unlock sustainable growth and create long-term value.

Value Creation & Impact Investing

We partner with businesses that demonstrate strong fundamentals and innovation to generate measurable financial, social and environmental impact for all stakeholders.

Strategic Partnerships
