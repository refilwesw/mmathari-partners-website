About Mmathari Partners

We are an investment firm specialising in private capital, dedicated to unlocking sustainable growth opportunities in Botswana and across Southern Africa through disciplined investing, strategic partnerships and responsible stewardship.

Who We Are

Mmathari Partners is focused on disciplined private capital investments in under-served segments. We identify opportunities that generate sustainable long-term value.

We partner with entrepreneurs, businesses, institutions and investors to deliver strategic capital, operational expertise and responsible governance.

Our approach combines commercial excellence with a commitment to impact and empowerment, ensuring that every investment is inclusive and contributes to economic growth, stronger businesses and resilient communities.

Our Vision

A trusted indigenous private markets investment partner, recognised for fostering empowerment, embedding a gender lens, championing diversity and delivering measurable impact to create sustainable value for businesses, investors and communities.

Our Mission

To catalyse growth by investing in exceptional businesses, creating value through active ownership and delivering measurable impact for investors, businesses and society.

What Sets Us Apart

Mmathari Partners combines disciplined investment expertise with a distinctive approach to value creation. We believe sustainable investment success is achieved by partnering closely with management teams, embedding responsible investment principles and creating measurable impact alongside strong financial performance.

Impact-Aligned Investing

We pursue investments that deliver competitive financial returns while generating meaningful social, environmental and economic outcomes.

Gender Lens Embedded

We embrace a gender lens investment approach, recognizing that greater inclusion and diversity contribute to stronger outcomes, enhanced business resilience, and long-term value creation.

Active Participation

We work alongside management teams as engaged partners, providing strategic guidance, governance support and operational expertise.

Sustainable Value Creation

We focus on building resilient businesses that create lasting value for investors, employees, customers and communities.
