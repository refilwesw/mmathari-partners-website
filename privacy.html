Privacy & Data Protection Statement

How Mmathari Partners handles personal information in connection with this website and related enquiries.

Privacy & Data Protection Statement

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Mmathari Partners respects the privacy of individuals and is committed to protecting personal information handled through this website and in the course of its business activities. This Statement explains, at a general level, how we collect, use, retain, disclose and protect personal information, and what rights you have.

1. Who We Are

Mmathari Partners is a Botswana-based private equity firm. For website enquiries, our contact details are: First Floor, Unit 1, Plot 75751, Setlhoa, Block 10, Gaborone, Botswana; telephone +2673216411; email info@mmatharipartners.co.bw.

2. Legal Framework

We process personal information in accordance with the Data Protection Act of 2024 and other laws that apply to us.

3. Personal Information We Collect

When you use the website's Contact form, you may provide your full name, email address, telephone number, subject and message. We may also receive information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with us by email, telephone or other business correspondence. We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website. Please do not include it in your messages.

Our website servers may automatically record technical information such as your date and time of your visit, for security and troubleshooting. This website does not currently use analytics, advertising cookies, newsletter sign-ups or online recruitment forms.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information only for specified and legitimate purposes and only where the Act gives us a lawful basis. Such personal information may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective investors, partners, entrepreneurs and other stakeholders, administer business relationships, maintain appropriate records, protect the security of our systems and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

5. Principles we follow

We aim to process personal information lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

We:

Collect it for specified, legitimate purposes and do not use it in ways that are incompatible with those purposes;

Collect only what is adequate, relevant and necessary;

Keep it accurate and correct or delete inaccurate information without undue delay;

Keep it no longer than necessary;

Protect it with appropriate security; and

Take responsibility for, and can demonstrate, our compliance with these principles.

6. Consent

We seek to collect information that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. We also seek to keep personal information accurate and to correct information where appropriate.

7. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where necessary to provide or support services, operate our business, comply with legal or regulatory requirements, protect our rights or security, or where disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by applicable law. Where third-party service providers process personal information on our behalf, we seek to require appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

8. International Transfers

Where personal information is transferred to or processed in another country, we will seek to apply safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. The appropriate safeguards will depend on the nature and circumstances of the transfer.

9. Security and Personal Data Breaches

We seek to apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. These include access controls, secure storage and staff confidentiality obligations. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

In case a personal data breach occurs; we will notify the Commissioner without undue delay and, where feasible, no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach as prescribed by the Act and communication to affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to put their rights and freedoms at risk, subject to the Act’s conditions.

10. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or record-keeping requirements.

11. Your Data Protection Rights

SSubject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to be informed about processing, request access, seek correction, request erasure, request restriction, object to certain processing, request portability where applicable, and exercise other rights provided by law.

Requests should be directed to info@mmatharipartners.co.bw. We may need to verify the identity of the requester before responding.

12. Photographs and Website Content

The website contains photographs of Mmathari Partners' team members, board members, investment committee and other corporate activities. These images are published as corporate website content. They should not be treated as a request for visitors to submit personal photographs or other personal information through the website..

13. Careers and Recruitment

The current Careers page does not provide an online CV-upload or recruitment application facility. If you contact Mmathari Partners regarding employment opportunities, information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, to assess or administer recruitment-related communications.

14. Investor Information

Investor-related information may be handled separately in connection with authorised investor communications and documentation. Access to investor materials is subject to the applicable terms, controls and confidentiality requirements governing those materials.

15. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This Statement does not make assumptions about cookies or analytics services that are not present in the website's current implementation. If tracking, analytics, advertising or other technologies are introduced that involve personal information, this Statement shall be reviewed and updated before or when those technologies are deployed, as appropriate.

16. Third-Party Websites and Services

The website may contain links to third-party services, including LinkedIn and external resources. Third-party websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Mmathari Partners is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that it does not control.

17. Children

This website is directed at professional and business audiences and is not intended to solicit personal information from children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through the website.

18. Changes to This Statement

We may update this Statement when our website, business practices or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date.

19. Contact Us

For privacy or data-protection enquiries, please contact Mmathari Partners at info@mmatharipartners.co.bw or +2673216411, or write to First Floor, Unit 1, Plot 75751, Setlhoa, Block 10, Gaborone, Botswana.

20. Applicable Law

TThis Statement is intended to support Mmathari Partners' compliance with applicable data-protection requirements in Botswana, including the Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024), which commenced on 14 January 2025. The Statement should be read together with applicable legislation and does not replace legal advice.
