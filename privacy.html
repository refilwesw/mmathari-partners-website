Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How Mmathari Partners handles personal information in connection with this website and related enquiries.

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Mmathari Partners respects the privacy of individuals and is committed to protecting personal information handled through this website and in the course of its business activities. This Policy explains, at a general level, how we collect, use, retain, disclose and protect personal information.

1. Who We Are

Mmathari Partners is a Botswana-based private equity firm. For website enquiries, our contact details are: First Floor, Unit 1, Plot 75751, Setlhoa, Block 10, Gaborone, Botswana; telephone +267 321 6411; email info@mmatharipartners.co.bw.

2. Personal Information We Collect

When you use the website's Contact form, you may provide your full name, email address, telephone number, subject and message. We may also receive information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with us by email, telephone or other business correspondence.

We do not state that this website collects information through analytics, advertising cookies, newsletters, online recruitment forms or other tracking technologies unless and until those functions are actually implemented.

3. How We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective investors, partners, entrepreneurs and other stakeholders, administer business relationships, maintain appropriate records, protect the security of our systems and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Lawful and Fair Processing

We aim to process personal information lawfully, fairly and transparently, for specified and legitimate purposes, and only to the extent reasonably necessary for those purposes. Where consent is relied upon, we will seek it in an appropriate manner and respect applicable rights to withdraw consent.

5. Data Minimisation and Accuracy

We seek to collect information that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. We also seek to keep personal information accurate and to correct information where appropriate.

6. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where necessary to provide or support services, operate our business, comply with legal or regulatory requirements, protect our rights or security, or where disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by applicable law. Where third-party service providers process personal information on our behalf, we seek to require appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

7. International Transfers

Where personal information is transferred to or processed in another country, we will seek to apply safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. The appropriate safeguards will depend on the nature and circumstances of the transfer.

8. Security

We seek to apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction or damage. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or record-keeping requirements.

10. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to be informed about processing, request access, seek correction, request erasure, request restriction, object to certain processing, request portability where applicable, and exercise other rights provided by law.

Requests should be directed to info@mmatharipartners.co.bw. We may need to verify the identity of the requester before responding.

11. Photographs and Website Content

The website contains photographs of Mmathari Partners' team members, board members, investment committee and other corporate activities. These images are published as corporate website content. They should not be treated as a request for visitors to submit personal photographs or other personal information through the website.

12. Careers and Recruitment

The current Careers page does not provide an online CV-upload or recruitment application facility. If you contact Mmathari Partners regarding employment opportunities, information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, to assess or administer recruitment-related communications.

13. Investor Information

Investor-related information may be handled separately in connection with authorised investor communications and documentation. Access to investor materials is subject to the applicable terms, controls and confidentiality requirements governing those materials.

14. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This Policy does not make assumptions about cookies or analytics services that are not present in the website's current implementation. If tracking, analytics, advertising or other technologies are introduced that involve personal information, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before or when those technologies are deployed, as appropriate.

15. Third-Party Websites and Services

The website may contain links to third-party services, including LinkedIn and external resources. Third-party websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Mmathari Partners is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that it does not control.

16. Children

This website is directed at professional and business audiences and is not intended to solicit personal information from children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through the website.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when our website, business practices or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date.

18. Contact Us

For privacy or data-protection enquiries, please contact Mmathari Partners at info@mmatharipartners.co.bw or +267 321 6411, or write to First Floor, Unit 1, Plot 75751, Setlhoa, Block 10, Gaborone, Botswana.

19. Applicable Law

This Policy is intended to support Mmathari Partners' compliance with applicable data-protection requirements in Botswana, including the Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024), which commenced on 14 January 2025. The Policy should be read together with applicable legislation and does not replace legal advice.
